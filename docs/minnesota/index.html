How good are the picks?

Each season was predicted by a model trained only on the other nine years. Using weather picks the best variety more often than choosing the variety with the best average yield. Predicting the overall yield level of a new season remains hard (R² close to 0), so read the ranking, not the exact tonnes.

What the model looks at

Mean absolute SHAP value: the average change in predicted yield (t/ha) caused by each input. Weather accounts for about three quarters of the model's explanation, variety for 17% and site for 8%.

Data: Minnesota barley trials and NOAA monthly weather, from the R package agridat (Wright 2013, The American Statistician 67: 129–133). Model: gradient boosting on variety, site and 14 weather variables. Historical varieties: this is a method demonstration, not advice for today's farms. Built by Saphal Sharma.
